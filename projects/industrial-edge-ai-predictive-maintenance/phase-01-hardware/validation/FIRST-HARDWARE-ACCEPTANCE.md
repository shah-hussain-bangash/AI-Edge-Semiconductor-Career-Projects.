## Baseline and priority channels

These are **proposed project acceptance targets**, not manufacturer accuracy guarantees or electrical operating limits. Exact received-part review and an adequate existing reference method are prerequisites. If reference uncertainty prevents resolving the target, mark the check BLOCKED, not PASS. No reference instrument is assumed to be present in the 30-product order.

| Check | Proposed physical pass criterion | Required evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1-B stationary baseline | At least 600 s elapsed; nominal 1 Hz summary stream. At least 594 valid samples for each of the three groups over the first 600 s; report actual rate, interval median/p95/max and gaps. No unexplained reset, duplicate identity, capture limit stop or interval above 3 s. Zero malformed, silently dropped or unaccounted records. | Original serial capture; start/stop and timing summary; exact count reconciliation including quarantined records. Never infer acquisition sampling rate from chart spacing. | PENDING |
| H1-A P03 acceleration/vibration group | Stationary readings at six documented orientations, at least 30 samples per orientation. Mean vector magnitude within 0.5 m/s² of declared local gravity reference (use 9.80665 m/s² only as a declared approximation); expected dominant axis changes sign on inversion, magnitude at least 8.8 m/s² and other axes at most 1.0 m/s² in magnitude. No clipping; all three axes finite with valid quality. Include mounting/alignment and reference uncertainty in review. | Per-axis readings in m/s², six labelled orientations and means, scale/configuration, valid/invalid counts and residuals. This is static/low-rate validation: the existing 1 Hz summaries cannot validate vibration spectra or predictive fault detection. | PENDING |
| H1-T P04 temperature | Two stable reference points, separated by at least 5 °C, within the exact probe's reviewed range and bench procedure. At least 30 samples per point; reference drift at most 0.2 °C during each window; absolute mean error plus reference expanded uncertainty at most 1.0 °C. No unexplained disconnect/startup placeholder accepted as a valid temperature. | Paired reference/sample readings in °C, stability window, probe placement, uncertainty and quality flags. No unreviewed heating method is specified. | PENDING |
| H1-I0 P05 current, open load path | With load terminals left disconnected under the approved sensor-only plan: at least 30 valid readings; absolute mean current plus reference expanded uncertainty at most 0.02 A. Preserve bus voltage in V as auxiliary evidence; no assumed live bus. | Original mA and mapped A values, exact shunt/calibration configuration, open-path inspection and zero-reference method. Convert mA to A exactly once. A zero-offset pass alone does not establish current accuracy. | PENDING |
| H1-I1 current scale, separate gate | **BLOCKED until** the existing parts support an independently reviewed safe reference procedure and separate authorization. Two distinct nonzero reference points, at least 30 readings each, below approved actual board/shunt/source/load ratings. Absolute mean error plus reference expanded uncertainty at most max(0.02 A, 5% of absolute reference current), with correct sign. No motor energization is included. | Approved reference method and safe scope, exact allowed point values/ratings, paired readings and uncertainty. Do not invent a load, borrow an unapproved supply or replace ordered hardware to close this row. | PENDING — dependency |
| H1-Q quality semantics | Approved nonhazardous negative case shows invalid/disconnected group maps to null, valid zero remains zero, and diagnostic reason survives conversion; no fabricated clock/session verification. Counts of accepted plus quarantined records equal raw parsed sample count. | Original flagged sample, offline candidate observation and quarantine/report entries, mapping comparison. Do not disconnect powered circuits to obtain a negative case. | PENDING |

The 594/600 target assumes the declared nominal 1 Hz summary configuration. A different actual configuration requires a new pre-approved count target; it is not a silent exception. Report total frames and valid counts separately. Capture boundaries and their timing uncertainty must be documented. The existing firmware loop may not satisfy this target: measure it and record a failure rather than claim an unmeasured rate.
