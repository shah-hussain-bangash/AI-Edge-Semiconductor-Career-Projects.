## Checks that block final component approval

1. **P6 motor:** obtain supplier/model documentation for normal, startup and stall current, mounting pattern and screw depth. Do not infer electrical current from an ambiguous advertised wattage.
2. **P7/P18/P19/P21/P23/P24:** coordinate adapter capacity, fuse time-current behavior, switch, driver, wiring and terminals with the verified motor. A fuse is not a substitute for driver current limiting.
3. **P2/P14:** verify actual ESP32 USB socket before buying the data cable. Current cart USB-C cable may not match a Micro-USB board.
4. **P25:** bracket must match the actual gearbox front mounting drawing. The complete JGA25 motor/bracket/controller kit inspected previously has no bracket-only option; do not duplicate the motor solely to obtain its bracket.
5. **P9/P10:** verify case and cooler fit as a pair. Use a separate official Pi supply.
6. **P20:** mount the motor and guard accessible rotating parts before operation.
7. **P22:** follow the final driver reference circuit. A single diode across a reversible H-bridge motor is not the default protection arrangement.
8. **P26–P29:** optional; buy only for an identified requirement. P30 spares can be satisfied by existing extra quantities.
